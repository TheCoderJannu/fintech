Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum CIBIL score required?

We generally recommend a minimum CIBIL score of 650 for the best interest rates. However, we have special schemes and collateral-based options for new-to-credit customers or those with lower scores.

Are there prepayment penalties?

For personal and floating-rate home loans, there are absolutely zero prepayment penalties. You can close your loan early and save on interest anytime without extra charges.

Do you offer loans for individuals with no credit history?

Yes. If you have no CIBIL score (-1 or 0), we can evaluate your profile based on your income stability, bank statement trends, or against a co-applicant.

What is the process for ITR filing through Navya Fintech?

Simply upload your Form 16 or Income statements in the 'Apply' section. One of our empaneled Chartered Accountants will contact you, maximize your deductions, and file it directly with the Income Tax Department within 24 hours.

Are there any upfront processing fees?

No. Navya Fintech never asks for upfront processing fees before your loan is approved. Any applicable processing fees are transparently deducted from your final disbursed loan amount.

Can I track my application status?

Yes! Once you apply, you will receive a WhatsApp message from your dedicated manager. You can ping them anytime for a live update on your file's status.
